Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Edge Betting Tools is a sports data and research software subscription. It is not a sportsbook, casino, or gambling operator. Edge does not accept wagers, hold betting funds, set odds, pay winnings, or place bets for users. You bet only on your own accounts at separate, licensed sportsbooks. Edge only links out, like an odds-comparison or sports news site. The price is a flat subscription no matter what you bet or win. 21+ only.

Who we are

This policy describes the personal information collected by Edge Betting Tools, operated by Dustin Smith in Mississippi, USA. It covers the Edge website and the software subscription. Contact support@edgebettingtools.com with privacy questions, including a request to delete your information.

Information we collect

  • Account email. We use it to create the account, send subscription notices, and reply to you.
  • Subscription status. We keep whether a subscription is in a trial, active, or canceled, so we can provide the software.
  • Payment, handled by Stripe. Stripe processes the subscription charge. Edge does not collect or store your card number or card security code. Stripe handles that payment data under Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Basic analytics. Pages visited, the referring page, browser and device type, and general region. We use this to understand how the site is used and to keep it working. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. You can control cookies in your browser.
  • Support email. If you write to support@edgebettingtools.com, we receive the address you write from and whatever you include in the message.

We do not ask for a phone number or a street address to use Edge. We do not use your information to accept wagers or to hold betting funds.

How we use it

We use this information to run the software subscription, bill through Stripe, send service messages such as trial, renewal, and cancellation notices, answer support and deletion requests, protect the service, and understand basic site usage.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with sportsbooks, and we do not share it for advertising sales. We share it only with service providers that help us run Edge, such as Stripe for payments, the company that hosts the site, and email delivery; when the law requires it; or when you ask us to. Those providers may use the information only to perform the service they provide to Edge.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while the account is open and for a limited time afterward when we need it for tax, accounting, security, or disputes. Support emails are kept long enough to handle the request and any follow-up.

Deletion requests

To ask us to delete your account email and the other personal information Edge holds about you, email support@edgebettingtools.com. Include the account email and say that you want it deleted. We will delete or de-identify the personal information we control. We may keep records the law requires us to keep, such as tax records. Stripe may keep payment records under its own policy and its legal duties. We reply by email to confirm the request.

Age

Edge is for people 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21. If you believe we have, email support@edgebettingtools.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date.
