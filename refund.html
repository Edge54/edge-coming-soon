Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Edge Betting Tools is a sports data and research software subscription. It is not a sportsbook, casino, or gambling operator. Edge does not accept wagers, hold betting funds, set odds, pay winnings, or place bets for users. You bet only on your own accounts at separate, licensed sportsbooks. Edge only links out, like an odds-comparison or sports news site. The price is a flat subscription no matter what you bet or win. 21+ only.

What the charge is for

The $69.99 USD monthly price is a flat fee for sports data and research software. It is not a wager, a deposit held for betting, or a share of winnings. The price does not change based on what you bet or win. Edge does not hold betting funds, so there is no betting balance to withdraw.

Access is delivered online for the subscription period. See the pricing page for what the plan includes and the Terms of Service for the full agreement.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel whenever you want. Email support@edgebettingtools.com and ask to cancel. We confirm by email.

After you cancel, access continues through the end of the period you already paid for. Cancellation stops the next renewal. It does not turn off access in the middle of a period you paid for.

5-day free trial

A new subscriber gets a 5-day free trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged. If the trial ends and you have not canceled, the subscription converts to the paid plan at $69.99 USD per month and renews each month until you cancel.

No partial-month refunds

We do not prorate or refund unused days in a billing period, except where the law requires a refund. If you cancel mid-month, you keep access until that paid period ends, and that period is not refunded.

Billing errors

If you were charged in error, we refund the billing error. That includes a duplicate charge, a charge after you canceled before the trial ended or before a renewal, and a charge that does not match the stated $69.99 USD price. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Email support@edgebettingtools.com and describe the charge.

How to request a cancellation or refund

Send the request to support@edgebettingtools.com. Use the email on the account, or include that address in the message, and say whether you want to cancel, report a billing error, or both. We reply by email. There is no phone line for billing.

Bets at sportsbooks are separate

Edge cannot refund a bet, a deposit, or a loss at a sportsbook. Edge did not take the wager and does not hold those funds. A sportsbook account is yours, at that sportsbook. Contact the sportsbook about that account.
